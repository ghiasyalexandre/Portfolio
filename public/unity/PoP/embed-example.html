Embedding example

The game below is placed with two lines. It fills its column width, keeps a 16:9 ratio and shows a click-to-play cover, so nothing downloads until a visitor chooses to play.

Snippet

<div data-plight-embed></div>
<script src="https://YOUR-HOST/plight/embed.js" defer></script>

Options: data-aspect="4/3", data-autoload="true", data-title="...", data-src="https://.../index.html". Size it with normal CSS on the container, e.g. a narrower one:

Plain iframe alternative

<iframe src="https://YOUR-HOST/plight/index.html" title="Plight of Proteus"
        allow="fullscreen; autoplay; gamepad; screen-wake-lock" allowfullscreen
        style="width:100%; aspect-ratio:16/9; border:0"></iframe>
